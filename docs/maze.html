Density Filter vs Density MPC

Maze 4 is the one default case where the one-step density filter collides while density MPC reaches the goal. The filter is greedy: it enforces a next-step density condition while staying close to the nominal command. In the dense room layout, that local correction is not enough to plan the turn sequence. MPC is predictive: it evaluates a short horizon of future states, so it can trade immediate tracking against future safety and steer through the room.
