Racing Density MPC

A track-coordinate racing example where density MPC-CDF tracks an L-shaped circuit while avoiding moving obstacle cars.

MPC-CDF Constraint

$$ \rho(x_{k+1})-\rho(x_k) +\Delta t\,\mathrm{div}(F_d)(x_k)\rho(x_k) -\Delta t\,C_k\rho(x_k)\ge 0 $$

Density and Track Cost

The racing example uses the same density-transport idea inside a nonlinear MPC. The objective balances track progress, state tracking, input effort, and density safety around moving obstacle cars.

Obstacle Density

Moving obstacle cars define local density bumps in the racing state space.

$$ \rho(x)=\frac{\Phi(x)}{V_{\mathrm{track}}(x)^\alpha} $$

MPC Decision Variables

The optimizer chooses a horizon of controls and density-rate variables.

$$ U=[u_0,\ldots,u_{N-1}], \qquad C=[C_0,\ldots,C_{N-1}] $$

Density Transport

Each predicted step enforces a discrete density condition.

$$ \rho(x_{j+1})-\rho(x_j)+\Delta t\,\mathrm{div}(F_d)\rho(x_j)-\Delta t\,C_j\rho(x_j)\ge 0 $$
