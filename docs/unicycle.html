Example family

Unicycle Safety Filters

Nonholonomic navigation with forward speed and yaw-rate controls. The examples compare density feedback, density filters, density MPC, and CBF-style safety filters.

Dynamics

$$ \begin{aligned} x_{k+1} &= x_k+\Delta t\,v_k\cos\theta_k,\\ y_{k+1} &= y_k+\Delta t\,v_k\sin\theta_k,\\ \theta_{k+1} &= \theta_k+\Delta t\,\omega_k. \end{aligned} $$

Density Construction

The density combines an attractive goal term with smooth obstacle bumps. The obstacle product \(\Phi(p)\) goes to zero near unsafe regions, while the denominator makes density grow near the goal.

Pose Density

The unicycle examples use position and heading error in the goal term.

$$ \rho(z)=\frac{\Phi(p)}{V(z)^\alpha}, \quad V(z)=(x-x_g)^2+(y-y_g)^2+w_\theta\,\mathrm{wrap}(\theta-\theta_g)^2 $$

Obstacle Product

Each obstacle contributes one smooth bump, and multiple obstacles multiply together.

$$ \Phi(p)=\prod_{i=1}^{M} b_i(p) $$

Circle Obstacle Example

For a circular obstacle with center \(c\), inner radius \(r_1\), and outer density radius \(r_2\), the distance is \(d(p)=\|p-c\|_2\).

Transition Coordinate

The value \(m(p)\) runs from 0 at the safety radius to 1 at the outer radius.

$$ m(p)=\frac{\|p-c\|_2^2-r_1^2}{r_2^2-r_1^2} $$

Smooth Bump

The bump is zero inside the safety radius, one outside the density radius, and smooth in between.

$$ b(p)= \frac{\exp(-1/m(p))} {\exp(-1/m(p))+\exp(-1/(1-m(p)))} $$

In the code, the agent radius inflates \(r_1\) and \(r_2\) before the controller evaluates these functions.

Controllers

Density Feedback

The planar density gradient gives a desired direction, which is converted to forward speed and heading tracking.

$$ u_\rho(p)=k_\rho\nabla\rho(p), \quad v=\|u_\rho\|, \quad \theta_d=\mathrm{atan2}(u_{\rho,y},u_{\rho,x}) $$

One-Step Density Filter

The filter stays close to \(u_{\mathrm{nom}}\) while enforcing the next-step density condition.

$$ \begin{aligned} \min_{u,s}\;&\frac{1}{2}\|u-u_{\mathrm{nom}}\|_W^2+\frac{1}{2}w_s\|s\|^2\\ \mathrm{s.t.}\;& \rho(z_{k+1})-\rho(z_k)+\Delta t\,\mathrm{div}(F_d)\rho(z_k)+s\ge 0 \end{aligned} $$

Density MPC

The MPC applies the density transport condition over a horizon with controls \(u_0,\ldots,u_{N-1}\).

$$ \rho(z_{j+1})-\rho(z_j)+\Delta t\,\mathrm{div}(F_d)\rho(z_j)-\Delta t\,C_j\rho(z_j)\ge 0 $$

CBF Baselines

The comparison scripts also include a discrete CLF-CBF filter and a short-horizon CBF MPC.

$$ h(z_{k+1})\ge (1-\gamma)h(z_k) $$
